# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis using Python

## Project Overview
This project focuses on performing **end-to-end sales analysis** on the AdventureWorks dataset using **Python**.  
The objective is to clean, merge, analyze, and visualize sales data to extract meaningful business insights related to **revenue, profit, products, customers, and regions**.

---

## Objectives
- Understand sales trends over time  
- Analyze product and category performance  
- Identify top customers and regions  
- Generate insights to support business decision-making  

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook  

---

## Dataset Information
- Dataset: AdventureWorks (public sample dataset)  
- Data Type: CSV files  
- Domains Covered:
  - Sales
  - Products & Categories
  - Customers
  - Territories
  - Calendar

---

## Project Workflow
1. Loaded multiple CSV datasets using Pandas  
2. Performed data cleaning and preprocessing  
3. Merged fact and dimension tables using left joins  
4. Created new features such as Year and Month  
5. Calculated key metrics like Revenue and Profit  
6. Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA)  
7. Created visualizations to identify trends and patterns  

---

## Key Analysis Performed
- Year-wise and month-wise revenue trends  
- Top products by revenue  
- Revenue and profit by product category  
- Regional sales performance  
- Top customers by revenue contribution  

---

## Visualizations
- Line charts for revenue trends  
- Bar charts for product and category comparison  
- Pie chart for category-wise revenue distribution  

All visualizations were created using **Matplotlib**.

---

## Key Insights
- Revenue shows consistent growth over the years  
- Certain product categories contribute a major share of revenue  
- A small group of products and customers generate high sales  
- Sales performance varies across regions
